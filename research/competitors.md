# FAA Part 107 Research — Competitor Analysis

## Major Players

### 1. Pilot Institute (pilotinstitute.com)
- **Price:** $159 one-time (lifetime access)
- **Pass rate:** 99.9%+, average score 92%
- **Content:**
  - 210+ video lessons (avg 3 min each)
  - 300+ practice questions, 20 quizzes
  - Unlimited practice exams
  - 14-page illustrated cheatsheet
  - Flashcard app (iOS/Android, 110 cards)
  - 13 audio review lessons
  - 14-Day Challenge structured study plan
- **Features:**
  - Lifetime access with updates
  - Mobile/offline access
  - Live office hours weekly
  - Facebook study group
  - Pass guarantee (refund test fee + course cost)
  - FAA Certified Flight Instructor (CFI) as instructor

### 2. Drone Pilot Ground School (dronepilotgroundschool.com)
- **Price:** $299 individual, school licenses $1,500-$3,000/yr
- **Pass rate:** 99%+
- **Content:**
  - 80+ video/text lessons
  - 30+ hours of content
  - 13 modules of curriculum
- **Modules:**
  1. Welcome (3 lessons)
  2. Drone Rules & FAA Regulations (11 lessons)
  3. Night Operations (4 lessons)
  4. National Airspace System (8 lessons)
  5. Reading Sectional Charts (10 lessons)
  6. Airport Operations (5 lessons)
  7. Weather & Micrometeorology (8 lessons)
  8. Drone Flight Operations (13 lessons)
  9. Practice Tests & Cram Sheet (6 lessons)
  10-12. Flight Skills (14 lessons)
  13. Workforce Development (7 lessons)
- **Features:**
  - Teacher/student separate portals
  - Progress tracking
  - Classroom activities
  - Flight simulator add-on ($200/student)
  - CTE/STEM aligned

### 3. UAV Coach (uavcoach.com)
- Similar to Drone Pilot Ground School
- 80+ video lessons
- Focus on step-by-step guidance

### 4. Free/Low-Cost Options
- YouTube: Multiple free study guide channels
- Quizlet: Free flashcard sets
- FAA official PDFs: Free
- iOS App Store: "FAA Part 107 Practice Test" (various)
- Google Play: "Part 107 Practice Test 2026"

## Common Pattern Across Successful Courses
1. **Structured modules** following ACS areas
2. **Short video lessons** (2-5 min)
3. **Practice exams** that mimic real test
4. **Flashcards** for terminology
5. **Cheatsheets / cram sheets** for review
6. **Progress tracking**
7. **Mobile access** (app or mobile web)
8. **Some form of pass guarantee**
9. **Instructor access** (live or async)
10. **Lifetime updates** for regulation changes

## Gaps/Opportunities
- Most are video-heavy; few are interactive app-first
- Pricing is $150-$300; room for a free or lower-cost alternative
- Few have gamification elements
- Most don't integrate well with flight planning tools
- Limited focus on practical flight skills (mostly academic)
- None are open-source or community-driven
